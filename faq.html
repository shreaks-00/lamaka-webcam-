🦙 Getting Started

What exactly is LAMAKA?

LAMAKA is a free, browser-based random video chat platform. Think of it as the upgraded, premium-tier successor to platforms like Omegle and Chatroulette. You click "Start Chatting," allow your camera, and within seconds you're connected face-to-face with a real stranger from anywhere in the world.

Unlike generic Omegle alternatives, LAMAKA is built with an obsessive focus on design quality, safety, and speed. The result is a video chat experience that feels premium rather than janky.

Is LAMAKA completely free? Any hidden costs?

100% free. No subscription. No premium tier. No in-app purchases. No hidden fees. LAMAKA will always be free for the core random video chat experience.

We sustain the platform through minimal, non-intrusive brand partnerships. We will never sell your data or put ads between you and your connections.

Do I need to create an account or sign up?

No account, no sign up, no email address required. LAMAKA is a true anonymous chat site. You open the page, click start, and you're in. Your session is temporary and leaves no trace.

This anonymity is fundamental to our design. We believe real, candid conversations happen when you're not worried about your social media followers seeing it.

Do I need to download anything?

Zero downloads required. LAMAKA is a web app that runs entirely inside your browser using WebRTC technology — the same technology powering Google Meet, Zoom web, and many other major video conferencing tools. It works on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge on both desktop and mobile.

📷 Camera & Microphone

Why does LAMAKA need camera and microphone access?

LAMAKA is a live webcam chat platform, so your camera and microphone are essential for the experience. When you click "Allow" on the browser permission prompt, your device's camera and mic stream is shared peer-to-peer directly with your matched partner.

Critically: your video stream is never routed through LAMAKA's servers. We never see, record, or store your video. It goes directly from your browser to your partner's browser using WebRTC encryption.

My camera isn't working. How do I fix it?

Camera issues are almost always a browser permissions problem. Here's how to fix it:

Chrome: Click the camera icon in the address bar → select "Always allow lamaka.chat to access camera and microphone" → reload the page.

Safari: Go to Safari → Settings → Websites → Camera, and set LAMAKA to "Allow."

Firefox: Click the camera/lock icon in the address bar → clear the blocked permission → reload.

If you're on a laptop and the above doesn't work, check your operating system camera privacy settings. On Windows, go to Settings → Privacy → Camera and ensure browser access is enabled.

Can I use LAMAKA without a camera?

Yes! You can choose "Simulate Without Camera" when prompted. You'll still be matched with live partners and can chat via text. Some partners may choose not to connect with camera-off users depending on their preference, but many text-only sessions happen every day on LAMAKA.

How do I turn my mic on and off during a chat?

There is a dedicated microphone toggle button in the video panel on your local camera feed (bottom panel in the full-screen view). Click the microphone icon to mute yourself — the icon turns red when muted. Click again to unmute. This works in real-time during any live session.

🌍 Matching & Connecting

How does the random matching algorithm work?

Our "Herd Algorithm" maintains a live pool of all currently available users. When you hit "Start Chatting," you're matched with a random user from that pool within seconds. The algorithm considers your gender preference filter (All, Girls Only, Guys Only) if you've set one.

Future versions will include interest-based matching, where you can enter tags like "music," "travel," or "coding" to be matched with compatible strangers.

Can I choose what country my matches come from?

Currently, matching is fully global — you can be connected with anyone from our 140+ supported countries. Country-specific filtering is on our roadmap for a future update. LAMAKA's global reach is actually one of its biggest strengths; many users report that their most memorable conversations have been with people from countries they never expected.

What does the "NEXT" button do?

The NEXT button instantly ends your current session and begins searching for a new random connection. There's no penalty for skipping. You can NEXT as many times as you like until you find someone you want to talk to.

🛡️ Safety & Privacy

Is LAMAKA safe to use?

LAMAKA employs multiple layers of protection. Our AI moderation system scans sessions for policy violations in real-time. Our one-click report button allows any user to instantly flag a bad actor. Violators are IP-banned and their device signatures are blocked from the platform permanently.

That said, as with any platform where strangers meet, common sense is your best tool. Never share personal information like your real name, address, school, or phone number with a stranger on LAMAKA. Read our full Safety Guide for more detailed advice.

Does LAMAKA record or save my video chats?

Absolutely not. LAMAKA does not record, store, or process your video or audio streams in any way. All video is transmitted peer-to-peer via WebRTC, meaning it flows directly between your browser and your partner's browser without passing through our servers. When the session ends, it's gone forever.

Is LAMAKA safe for minors?

LAMAKA is intended for users aged 18 and over. We strongly discourage minors from using the platform, as random video chat with strangers carries inherent risks for younger users. If you are a parent concerned about a child's use of LAMAKA, we recommend using browser parental controls to restrict access. Report any suspected underage activity via our report button and it will be actioned immediately.
