Our Story

LAMAKA was born in 2026 from a simple frustration: the world's most popular stranger chat apps had become cesspools of bots, ads, and poor user experience. The original Omegle-era apps had a beautiful core concept — connect two random strangers instantly — but failed their communities through neglect.

Our founding team of designers, engineers, and community builders set out to create something genuinely different. We spent months obsessing over every detail — from the soft gold color palette (inspired by premium metal craftsmanship) to the sub-50ms WebRTC connection architecture. We wanted every interaction on LAMAKA to feel like you've just unlocked a VIP lounge.

The name "LAMAKA" is a playful nod to the llama — an animal known for being social, surprisingly intelligent, and occasionally dramatic. Much like the best conversations you'll have on our platform.

Our Commitment to Safety

Safety is not an afterthought at LAMAKA — it's architecture. Every feature decision we make is filtered through a "could this be abused?" lens. Here's how we protect our community:

  • Zero Data Storage: We do not record, store, or sell your video or audio streams. Your conversations disappear the moment the session ends.
  • One-Click Report: Every session has a prominently placed report button. Reports are actioned by our moderation team within minutes, 24/7.
  • IP-Level Bans: Violators are permanently IP-banned. Repeat offenders face device-level blocks.
  • Transparent Community Guidelines: We publish clear, plain-language rules. No legal jargon that hides what we actually do.
  • Encrypted Signaling: All connection handshakes are TLS-encrypted. We never see your private communications.
