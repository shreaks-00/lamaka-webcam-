LAMAKA Safety Guide

Chatting with strangers online can be fun and enriching. Here's everything you need to know to do it safely on LAMAKA.

🛡️ Our Safety Architecture

At LAMAKA, safety is not a policy checkbox — it's infrastructure. Every technical and product decision we make is filtered through the lens of user protection. Here is how our safety systems work at a technical level:

  • Peer-to-Peer WebRTC: Your video and audio streams never touch our servers. They travel directly from your browser to your partner's browser using encrypted WebRTC channels. We cannot intercept, record, or view your sessions even if we wanted to.
  • TLS Signaling Encryption: All connection metadata (matching, session initialization) is transmitted over TLS 1.3 — the same encryption standard used by banks and major e-commerce platforms.
  • Zero Persistent Logs: When your session ends, its metadata is purged from our temporary signaling servers within 60 seconds. We maintain no long-term logs of who you chatted with, when, or for how long.
  • Real-Time AI Moderation: Our computer vision moderation layer monitors session thumbnails (never full video) for flagged content categories. If a violation pattern is detected, the session is auto-terminated and the offending user's IP is banned.
  • Community Reporting: Every session has a one-click report button. Our moderation team responds to all reports within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

✅ The Golden Rules of Safe Stranger Chat

No platform can protect you if you share sensitive information yourself. These rules apply universally when chatting with strangers online, whether on LAMAKA or anywhere else:

🚫
Never Share Personal Info
Your full name, home address, school name, workplace, phone number, and financial details should NEVER be shared with a stranger online. Treat every new connection as completely anonymous until proven otherwise over a long period of trust.
📍
Guard Your Location
Avoid mentioning specific neighborhoods, nearby landmarks, or your city unless you are comfortable with that level of sharing. Even seemingly innocent location details can be combined with other information to identify you.
📸
Think Before You Show
Be mindful of what is visible behind you in your camera frame. Family photos, diplomas with your name, identifiable street views through windows, or anything personally identifiable could be screenshotted and misused.
💬
Never Move Off-Platform Quickly
Bad actors often try to move conversations off-platform quickly (to WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.) before LAMAKA's protections apply. Be cautious of any stranger pushing aggressively to get your personal messaging handles immediately.
👩‍💻
Use a VPN if Concerned
Although LAMAKA does not expose your IP address to other users, using a VPN adds an additional layer of anonymity at the network level. Services like NordVPN or ProtonVPN are widely trusted options.
🔇
Use Your Mute Button
Use the microphone toggle button in your local camera panel to mute yourself at any time. If background conversations in your home contain personal information, muting proactively is always a smart move.

⚠️ Warning Signs — What to Watch For

🚨

If anyone on LAMAKA exhibits any of the following behaviors, use the NEXT button immediately and report them. You do not owe a stranger an explanation for ending a conversation.

  • Asking for your real name, age, address, phone number, school, or social media within the first few minutes.
  • Pressuring you to move the conversation to another platform (WhatsApp, Snapchat, Telegram) very early.
  • Displaying or requesting sexual content — this is a strict violation of our community guidelines.
  • Making threatening, racist, or hateful statements.
  • Claiming to be in distress or an emergency as a pretext to get your personal contact details.
  • Attempting to get you to click on external links, which could lead to phishing or malware sites.
  • Any behavior that makes you feel uncomfortable, regardless of whether it fits one of the above categories — trust your gut.

📋 How to Report a User

Reporting bad actors is the single most impactful thing you can do to keep LAMAKA safe for everyone. Here is how to do it in the current version:

  • Step 1: While in any session, click the NEXT button to end the conversation with the violating user and protect yourself immediately.
  • Step 2: After clicking NEXT, a brief report prompt will appear. Select the violation category (Harassment, Explicit Content, Spam/Bot, Hate Speech, Other).
  • Step 3: Submit the report. Our system logs the violating user's session data (IP, timestamp, session ID) and sends it to our moderation queue.
  • Step 4: Our team reviews all reports within 15 minutes. Confirmed violators are permanently IP-banned and device-fingerprint blocked.
✅

Reports are anonymous. The user you report will never know you reported them. We protect reporters absolutely.

👧 Guidance for Parents

LAMAKA is designed for adults aged 18 and over. We understand that curious teenagers may attempt to access the platform regardless. Here is our guidance for parents:

  • Use Browser Parental Controls: Most modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) support parental control extensions and content filtering. Block lamaka.chat in your parental control software if you wish to prevent access.
  • Have the Conversation: Educate teenagers on the general risks of talking to strangers online. Our Golden Rules above are an excellent framework for that conversation — with or without LAMAKA specifically.
  • Report Underage Users: If you suspect a user on the platform is under 18, report them immediately using our in-session report tool. We take these reports very seriously and will investigate and remove underage users.

We are committed to working with parent advocacy groups to improve our minor-protection policies continuously.
