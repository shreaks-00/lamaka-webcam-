1. Overview & Our Privacy Philosophy

LAMAKA Inc. ("LAMAKA", "we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and operating with radical transparency about our data practices. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information when you use lamaka.chat and related services.

Our core privacy philosophy is simple: the less data we have about you, the less we can lose, sell, or misuse. We have engineered LAMAKA from day one to operate on minimal data collection. We do not require account registration, we do not ask for your name or email, and we do not store your video or audio under any circumstances.

This policy applies to all visitors and users of LAMAKA's web properties. By using LAMAKA, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Given our anonymous-first architecture, we collect an absolute minimum of data. Here is a complete and transparent accounting of everything we collect:

  • IP Address (Temporary): When you visit LAMAKA, our servers temporarily log your IP address for security and abuse prevention purposes. This is used to enforce bans and detect coordinated bot attacks. IP logs are automatically purged within 24 hours of session termination. We do not use IP addresses for advertising targeting.
  • Session Metadata (Temporary): To facilitate WebRTC matchmaking, our signaling server briefly stores: a temporary session ID, approximate timestamp of session start and end, and your gender filter preference (if set). This data is deleted within 60 seconds of session termination.
  • Browser & Device Information (Anonymous): We collect anonymous browser type, operating system, and screen resolution data to optimize platform performance. This data is aggregated and cannot be traced back to an individual user.
  • Cookies (Functional Only): We use session cookies exclusively for platform functionality — specifically to maintain your active session state and gender filter preference within a single browser session. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party cookie scripts beyond Google Fonts CDN (which has its own privacy policy).
  • Moderation Reports: When you submit a report via our in-session report tool, we log: the reporter's temporary session ID, the reported user's temporary session ID, and the report category selected. This data is retained for 90 days for abuse pattern analysis, then permanently deleted.

What we do NOT collect: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, physical address, payment information, social media profiles, contacts, or location beyond broad country-level geolocation (used only for server routing optimization, not stored). We never collect biometric data.

3. Video & Audio Data

This is our most important privacy commitment. LAMAKA does not collect, transmit to our servers, record, store, or process your video or audio streams in any form.

All video chat on LAMAKA uses WebRTC (Web Real-Time Communication), a browser standard that enables direct peer-to-peer media streaming. Your video travels directly from your browser to your matched partner's browser using encrypted DTLS-SRTP channels. LAMAKA's servers act only as signaling brokers (to initiate the connection handshake) and do not participate in, intercept, or proxy the media stream itself.

The technical implication of this architecture is that it is physically impossible for us to record your video even if we wanted to — we never receive the media stream on our infrastructure.

4. How We Use Your Data

The minimal data we collect is used exclusively for the following operational purposes:

  • Platform Operation: Matching you with other available users, establishing WebRTC connection sessions, and maintaining session state during an active chat.
  • Safety & Abuse Prevention: Detecting and blocking malicious actors, enforcing bans, investigating moderation reports, and identifying coordinated bot or spam activity.
  • Performance Optimization: Routing connections through the optimal server region for lowest latency, and analyzing aggregate (non-identifiable) usage patterns to improve platform stability.
  • Legal Compliance: Maintaining minimum required records to comply with applicable law and to respond to valid legal process from governmental authorities.

We never use your data for advertising targeting, behavioral profiling, cross-site tracking, selling to data brokers, or any commercial purpose beyond the above.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third party for any commercial purpose. Period.

We use the following limited third-party services in the operation of LAMAKA:

  • Google Fonts: We load fonts from Google Fonts CDN. Google's standard CDN logging may apply. No LAMAKA user data is shared with Google.
  • STUN/TURN Servers: WebRTC connection establishment uses industry-standard STUN/TURN relay infrastructure to traverse firewalls. These servers see only connection metadata (IP, port), never media content.
  • Content Delivery Network (CDN): Static assets (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) may be served via a CDN provider. CDN providers receive only anonymized request logs.

We may disclose your data to law enforcement authorities only if required by a valid court order, subpoena, or equivalent legal process, and only to the extent required to comply with that legal obligation.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you. Given our minimal data collection architecture, this will typically be an empty or near-empty response.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request deletion of any data we hold. Due to our automatic 24-hour and 60-second purge cycles, most data is already deleted before such a request would be processed.
  • Right to Object: You may object to any processing of your data. Contact us at privacy@lamaka.chat to submit an objection.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: EU/EEA users have the right to lodge a complaint with their national data protection authority.
  • CCPA Rights: California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell it).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@lamaka.chat. We respond to all valid data requests within 30 days.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, for material changes, we will display a notice on the LAMAKA homepage for 30 days after the change takes effect.

Your continued use of LAMAKA after any privacy policy change constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms. If you disagree with any change, you must discontinue use of the Platform.
